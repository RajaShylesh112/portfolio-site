Here’s a modified structure and UX plan for your blog content:

🛠️ Modified Blog Content Structure (Writing-Heavy Version)
Each post should feel like a short case study, journal, or essay. This is how to structure it:

1. Title
Be clear and specific. Example:
“Designing a Cache Strategy for My Student Project”
or
“Why I Ditched MongoDB for PostgreSQL in My Appwrite Backend”

2. Meta Info
📅 Date

🏷️ Tags: System Design, Web Dev, College Life, Infra, etc.

🧱 Level: Beginner, Student Project, Deep Dive, etc.

3. Opening Hook (1-2 short paragraphs)
Tell the story behind the post.

What triggered this exploration?

What were you trying to build?

Where were you stuck?

“While building a project for a CS assignment, I realized my login system was trash. Tokens were getting reused. This led me down a rabbit hole of session storage.”

4. Section 1: Background
Give a little theory or problem setup.

Explain the system or technology involved.

Introduce terms or constraints if needed.

5. Section 2: What I Tried
Show what you attempted.

Describe the design tradeoffs or decisions.

Show code only if it’s essential (link to full repo otherwise).

6. Section 3: Where It Failed or Worked
Be brutally honest.

Share what didn't work and why.

Highlight your “a-ha” moment or insights.

7. Section 4: The Final Decision / Design
What approach did you finally use?

Why?

Include diagrams or simplified visuals if needed.

8. Section 5: What I Learned
Reflect. This is gold. Just 3–5 bullet points on:

Tech takeaways

Personal dev process insights

Stuff you’d do differently

9. Section 6: References / Next Steps
Link articles, repos, docs

Mention if you'll follow this up with another blog

Or ask readers to give feedback on a specific part

✍️ Optional Writing Add-ons
A quote at the top that reflects the vibe of the post.

Footnotes or expandable explanations (for deeper concepts).

A sidebar with “Related Posts” or “See also” (UX bonus).

🧠 UX Considerations (For Writing-Heavy Content)
Sticky table of contents on the side for each post (auto-generated from headers).

Estimated read time under each title (7 min read).

Keyboard scroll between articles (← and →).

Highlight your favorite posts as Editor’s Picks.
